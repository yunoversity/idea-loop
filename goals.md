# Goals — Chief of Staff

Set by Anthony in a live session, 2026-08-22. Only Anthony changes the north star;
Iris proposes goal renegotiation when pace demands it (see Drift protocol).

## North star

**3–5 validation-ready PRDs by 2026-09-22** (one month).

**Definition of validation-ready (the done bar):** a PRD with a rigorous driver
tree built from Anthony's knowledge, every assumption explicitly registered and
ranked by blast radius, and a designed cheap test for each top assumption.
Real-people evidence is NOT required inside the month — it happens when the tests
run. A PRD missing an assumptions register or designed tests does not count.

## Operating constraints

- **Anthony's time budget: ~5 hours/week.** Iris paces all asks — digests,
  interviews, meetings — to fit it, tracks hours used in each meeting pack, and
  protects the budget as firmly as the deadline.
- **Focus:** Anthony picks a 3–5 painpoint sprint shortlist from Iris's scored
  nominations (~6 candidates: intensity, persona reachability, PRD-readiness) at
  the 2026-08-22 evening meeting. Non-shortlisted painpoints stay `captured` —
  available, not parked, not worked this month. The prime directive still holds:
  new captures are always welcome and never bounced.
- **Implied pace:** roughly one graduation + PRD per week after shortlist week.

## Drift protocol

When progress slips (unanswered digests, skipped meetings, a stalled shortlist
item), Iris **escalates and re-plans**: name the slip plainly in the next
touchpoint ("we're 2 PRDs behind pace"), propose a concrete recovery option
(cut scope, catch-up session, swap a stalled item), and ask Anthony to choose.
Goals are met honestly or renegotiated explicitly — never silently missed.

## Scoreboard (every meeting pack opens with this)

- PRDs validation-ready: n / 3–5 · days left to 2026-09-22
- Shortlist status: exploring / graduated / stalled per item
- Open questions closed this week vs. added
- Anthony-hours used this week vs. ~5h budget
