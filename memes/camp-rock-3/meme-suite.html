The Receipts

Every meme above is load-bearing on one of these verified behind-the-scenes facts. Nothing was invented; only exaggerated with love.

16 yrsWait since Camp Rock 2
7 wksActual shoot length
~119:1Wait-to-shoot ratio
8Executive producers
5Final Jam "winners"
  • Filming: Sept 15 – Nov 1, 2025, Vancouver, Canada — a seven-week autumn shoot for a summer-camp movie. Released August 2026 on Disney Channel and Disney+.
  • Crew: directed by Veronica Rodriguez (The Slumber Party), written by Eydie Faye (Fuller House); EPs include all three Jonas Brothers, Demi Lovato, and Tim Federle.
  • Premise: Connect 3 lose their reunion-tour opening act at the last minute and recruit a replacement via a summer-camp talent competition.
  • Ending: Mitchie convinces Connect 3 they "don't have to pick just one" — all five finalists win Final Jam and share the opening slot.
  • Demi's cameo was kept secret until the world premiere; in it Mitchie takes a tour break to watch the camp show and repeats her most-quoted 2008 line, "She's really good." She once joked publicly about wanting an R-rated Camp Rock 3.
  • Villain: influencer Madison, flanked by cronies Daniel and Danielle — carbon copies of Tess Tyler's 2008 backup singers.
  • Cameo #2: Milo Manheim as Calvin Rhodes, yet another world-famous Camp Rock alum, hosting a songwriting workshop ("dig deep").
  • Callbacks: the film opens on a "Brand New Day" cover with campers flipping off the bus (shot-for-shot 2008 homage) and closes on "We Rock."
  • Director's forecast: new lead Malachi Barton learned guitar for the role; Rodriguez predicts he'll be "the next Zac Efron."
  • Full circle: Joe Jonas, watching the new campers with Demi: "pinching ourselves like wow, this is a pretty wild full circle moment."
