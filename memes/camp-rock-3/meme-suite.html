The Receipts

Every meme above is load-bearing on one of these verified details. Nothing was invented; only exaggerated with love (and one corkboard of red string).

  • Premiere: August 13, 2026 on Disney Channel — the cable channel, in 2026 — with Disney+ the next day; the 17-track soundtrack dropped Aug 14 via Walt Disney Records.
  • The tracklist confession: new cast songs include "Work In Progress," "Echo," "Rhythm's Only Right," and "One Beat Away"; the Jonas Brothers' single new contribution is "Come On Over," plus a "We Rock" remake whose vocals TikTok pronounced "insane."
  • Filming: Sept 15 – Nov 1, 2025, Vancouver — a seven-week late-autumn shoot for a summer-camp movie, supervised by eight executive producers (Joe, Nick, Kevin, Demi, Tim Federle, Betsy Sullenger, Spencer Berman, Gary Marsh). More EPs than weeks.
  • Premise: Connect 3 lose their reunion-tour opening act at the last minute and source a replacement from a children's talent competition at their childhood camp.
  • Ending: Mitchie tells Connect 3 they "don't have to pick just one" — all five finalists (Sage, Fletch, Rosie, Desi, Cliff) win Final Jam and share the tour slot.
  • The cameo: Demi Lovato's Mitchie return was kept secret until the world premiere; on screen she takes a tour break to watch the camp show and repeats her most-quoted 2008 line, "She's really good." The cast later performed that line four ways (supportive, angry, disappointed, sarcastic) on TikTok. Demi had once publicly floated an R-rated Camp Rock 3.
  • Villain: influencer Madison, flanked by cronies named Daniel and Danielle — carbon copies of Tess Tyler's 2008 backup singers.
  • Cameo #2: Milo Manheim as Calvin Rhodes, yet another world-famous Camp Rock alum, hosting a songwriting workshop ("dig deep") at a camp that now out-produces every conservatory on Earth.
  • The prophecy: director Veronica Rodriguez says new lead Malachi Barton — who learned guitar for the movie — will be "the next Zac Efron."
  • Full circle: Joe Jonas, watching the new campers with Demi: "pinching ourselves like wow, this is a pretty wild full circle moment."
