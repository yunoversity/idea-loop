# Camp Rock 3 — behind-the-scenes research dossier

Compiled 2026-08-23 to ground the meme suite in real, verifiable specifics.
(Off-pipeline fun project — not a painpoint, touches no pipeline state.)

## Production facts

- Released August 2026 on Disney Channel and Disney+ — 16 years after
  *Camp Rock 2: The Final Jam* (2010).
- Principal photography: **September 15 – November 1, 2025, in Vancouver,
  Canada** — a seven-week autumn shoot for a movie set at an American
  summer camp.
- Director: Veronica Rodriguez (*The Slumber Party*). Writer: Eydie Faye
  (*Fuller House*).
- **Eight executive producers**: Joe, Nick, and Kevin Jonas, Demi Lovato,
  Tim Federle (*HSMTMTS*), Betsy Sullenger, Spencer Berman, Gary Marsh.
- Demi Lovato returned as EP plus a **secret Mitchie cameo** hidden from
  fans until the world premiere. She had previously joked publicly about
  wanting an R-rated Camp Rock 3.
- Director Rodriguez on new lead Malachi Barton (learned guitar for the
  role): he'll be "the next Zac Efron."
- Joe Jonas on set with Demi watching the new campers: "both were looking
  at these new campers kind of pinching ourselves like wow, this is a
  pretty wild full circle moment."

## Plot / premise facts

- Premise: Connect 3 (Shane, Nate, Jason Gray) lose their reunion-tour
  opening act at the last minute, so they return to their childhood summer
  camp and recruit teenagers via a talent competition.
- Final Jam ending: Mitchie convinces Connect 3 they "don't have to pick
  just one" — **all five finalists win** (Sage, Fletch, Rosie, Desi, Cliff)
  and jointly become the tour's opening act.
- Mitchie's cameo: she "takes a break from her tour" to attend the kids'
  camp talent show, reminisces backstage with her mom Connie
  (Maria Canals-Barrera returning), and delivers "She's really good" — a
  callback to her most-quoted 2008 line.
- Villain: Madison, an "intimidating influencer," with cronies Daniel and
  Danielle — carbon copies of Tess Tyler's backup singers from 2008.
- Milo Manheim cameos as Calvin Rhodes, a famous Camp Rock alum, running a
  songwriting workshop where he tells campers to "dig deep."
- Opening montage: campers do flips off the bus to a cover of "Brand New
  Day" (shot-for-shot 2008 homage); the movie closes on "We Rock."
- New campers: Sage (courageous/determined), her "easygoing" brother Desi,
  cello prodigy Rosie, rebellious guitarist Fletch, Cliff, choreography
  queen Callie.

## v2 addendum (2026-08-23) — soundtrack & premiere-week specifics

- Premiere: **Aug 13, 2026 on Disney Channel** (linear first!), Disney+ the
  next day. 17-track soundtrack released Aug 14 via Walt Disney Records.
- New cast songs include **"Work In Progress"** (yes, really, on the movie
  that took 16 years), "Echo," "Rhythm's Only Right," "One Beat Away"
  (single, July 1), "Play My Music" remake (Aug 7). Five legacy songs were
  re-recorded by the new generation.
- The Jonas Brothers contributed **exactly one new song, "Come On Over,"**
  plus a "We Rock" remake — TikTok discourse called the finale vocals
  "insane."
- Cast TikTok: Segura, Pollack, Stone, and Barton performed "She's really
  good" in four registers — supportive, angry, disappointed, sarcastic.
