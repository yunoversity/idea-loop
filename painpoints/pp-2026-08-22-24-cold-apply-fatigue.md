# Cold-apply fatigue: job hunters need genuine direct outreach in their networks

## Problem
Job hunters are tired of cold applying and need to focus on direct outreach in
their networks instead — but the tooling path is blocked in both directions.

## Detailed pains
- Cold applications feel exhausting and low-yield; the hunter knows network
  outreach works better but it's high-effort per contact.
- Sites like LinkedIn don't allow AI scraping, so automated network-mapping
  approaches are off the table (ToS constraint, not just technical).
- AI-generated templated messages don't come across as genuine or effortful
  when received by hiring managers — the automation that exists poisons the
  very signal (care, effort) that makes outreach work.

## Existing workarounds
- Cold applying anyway (the thing they're tired of).
- Manual, hand-crafted outreach messages (works but doesn't scale with a
  full-time search).
- AI-templated outreach blasts (backfires: reads as low-effort to recipients).
