## Design constraints (fixed at capture)

1. **No LinkedIn scraping.** ToS constraint, treated as hard. Any approach
   requiring automated scraping of LinkedIn is out of scope — the MVP must work
   from data the user brings (their own contacts, exports they're entitled to,
   memory prompts) or public non-restricted sources.
2. **The genuineness paradox is assumption #1.** The painpoint itself says
   AI-templated messages read as fake. Any AI-assisted outreach product risks
   embodying the problem it critiques. The MVP's first falsifiable claim must
   be: *there exists an AI-assisted workflow whose output recipients judge as
   genuine and effortful.* If that fails, no feature list saves the idea.
   Candidate framings to explore (Socrates/Athena, not decided): AI as
   researcher/briefing tool while the human writes; AI as draft-critic
   ("this sounds templated") rather than draft-writer; AI for targeting/
   sequencing while message stays human.
